The PDF editor that stays on your computer

Elanero reads, edits, reviews, organizes, redacts, recognizes, converts and signs PDF files, all on your own PC. No account, no subscription, no advertising, no telemetry.

Features

One app for the whole life of a PDF

Open a file to read it, then change it, review it, protect it or turn it into something else. Each card links to the matching page of the user guide.

  • Read and search

    Tabs and separate windows, four page layouts, thumbnails, bookmarks and night reading. Search the whole document with whole-word, case and regular-expression options, then print with a preview.

    Reading and navigation →

  • Edit text and images

    Change existing paragraphs in place, keeping the original fonts where possible. Add text, pictures and shapes; move, resize or replace images. Every edit can be undone.

    Editing text and images →

  • Comment and review

    Highlight, underline and strike out text. Add notes, text boxes, callouts, shapes, freehand ink and stamps, including your own image stamps. Reply to and filter comments in one panel.

    Comments and review →

  • Organize pages

    Reorder, rotate, delete, extract and insert pages with drag and drop. Combine several files into one or split a document into parts. Add page numbers, headers, footers and watermarks.

    Pages →

  • Redact and protect

    Remove text, areas or whole pages permanently, with a check that nothing is left underneath. Clean out hidden data such as metadata and scripts, and encrypt files with AES-256 passwords.

    Redaction and security →

  • OCR for scans

    Turn scanned pages into searchable, selectable text while the scan stays as it looks. English is built in, other languages are a free download, and several files can be recognized in one batch.

    OCR and scanned documents →

  • Export and convert

    Save as Word, Excel, HTML, Markdown, plain text or PNG, JPEG and TIFF images. The conversions run inside the app: no Office, and no conversion service.

    Export and conversion →

  • Optimize and compare

    See what takes up space in a file and make it smaller, with an estimate before anything is written. Compare two versions side by side, down to changed words and visual differences.

    Optimize and compare →

  • PDF/A and accessibility

    Check a document against common PDF/A rules and convert it to PDF/A-2b for archiving. Check tagging, language, alternative text, reading order and contrast, and fix what can be fixed.

    PDF/A and accessibility →

  • Digital signatures

    Sign with a certificate file, or with a USB token or smart card through the Windows certificate store, as a visible or invisible PAdES signature. Place a signature box for each person who has to sign, and see whether the signatures in a file are intact.

    Digital signatures →

  • Translate as you read

    Select text and see it in another language in a bubble beside it. Translation uses an online service, so it is off until you turn it on, and only the text you select is sent.

    Translation →

  • Works the way you do

    Light and dark themes, a command palette that finds any command by name, and keyboard shortcuts you can change.

    Preferences →

Not everything is here yet: creating fillable forms, for example, is not supported. The README keeps an honest list of limitations and feature status.

Privacy

Your documents are not our business

Contracts, invoices and medical letters are exactly what people open in a PDF editor. Elanero processes them on your computer and sends them nowhere.

Privacy policy Privacy in the user guide

  • Everything runs locally Editing, OCR, conversion, redaction and signing happen on your PC. Your files stay where you saved them.
  • No account and no telemetry No sign-in, analytics, advertising or automatic crash reports. This page loads nothing from other sites either.
  • Online only when you ask, with one exception Downloading an OCR language, requesting a timestamp for a signature, or translating text you selected. The exception: the Microsoft Store version checks the Store for updates once a day, which you can turn off in Preferences. The privacy policy lists each case.
  • Open source you can check The complete source code is public under the AGPL-3.0-or-later license.
